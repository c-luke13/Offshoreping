The Problem

Swimmers are invisible beyond the shore

Open water has no cellular coverage. When a swimmer gets into difficulty, there is no reliable way to locate them remotely — and existing tools weren't built with swimmers in mind.

🌊

No signal, no trace

Cellular networks end at the waterline. GSM trackers fail the moment a swimmer goes offshore — exactly where they're needed most.

⏱️

Found hours later

Rescue services frequently locate missing swimmers only after prolonged searching — when rapid response would have made the difference.

❌

Existing solutions fail

VHF radios are bulky and one-way. Satellite devices cost hundreds of euros. Nothing is built to be worn by a swimmer during training or competition.

"As a swimmer and surfer, I have directly witnessed cases where people went missing in open water and were only found hours later." — Luke Coleman, Founder
The Solution

OffshorePing closes the loop

A wearable device on the swimmer communicates directly with a shore-based monitoring station over long-range radio — infrastructure-free, reliable, and built for the water.

1

Swimmer presses button

Emergency alert or position update triggered from the wearable — mid-stroke, offshore, no phone needed.

2

GPS position transmitted

Live coordinates sent over long-range radio up to 5 km — no cellular infrastructure required.

3

Shore station receives

Onshore monitor logs position, raises alerts, and displays live map for coaches or safety staff.

4

Swimmer is confirmed

An acoustic signal on the wearable confirms the message was received — the swimmer knows they're tracked.

🔔 The ACK Feedback Loop

No existing product closes the loop back to the swimmer. OffshorePing's patented acknowledgement system gives the swimmer in-water confirmation that their position has been received — a first in open-water safety.

Wearability

Designed for the water

Four attachment configurations — all waterproof, all maintaining antenna orientation, none restricting movement.

🏊

Elastic band

Routed across swim cap exterior, secured at both ends. Distributes pressure evenly, stable during all swimming strokes.

🥽

Goggle strap clip

Bracket slots onto lateral goggle strap. Device co-located with goggles at rear of head. Zero cap interference.

🧢

Cap edge clip

Rigid clip grips the folded edge of the swim cap. Simple, tool-free attachment and removal.

🌊

Wetsuit pocket

Zip pocket at upper back of wetsuit. Positions antenna above waterline. Built for surfers at Nazaré, Peniche, and Ericeira.
